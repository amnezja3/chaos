Data sources

The viewer simulates a camera using an archival image; it is not a live stream. The game camera position and photograph position are separate data.

Pilot material: an AI-generated fictional Paris street made for CHAOS. It does not document the selected location and has no assigned Mapillary photography license.

The Mapillary adapter is prepared for a controlled connection. Acquisition and serving require separate approval and resolution of retention and linking conditions. This page does not imply active public access.

Each served provider image carries its own author, source, license and transformation details in the image panel. This page does not replace per-image attribution. Player photo uploads are not supported.
